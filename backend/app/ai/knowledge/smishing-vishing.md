# Smishing and Vishing

Smishing is phishing by SMS or messaging apps. Vishing is voice phishing by phone call, sometimes using AI-generated voices. Both rely on urgency and trust, just like email phishing.

Typical smishing messages claim a parcel delivery problem, a bank alert, or a prize, and include a short link. Do not tap the link. Open the official app or type the website address yourself.

In a vishing call, the caller may claim to be from IT support, a bank or a government agency and ask you to read out a code, install remote-access software or confirm personal details. Hang up and call back on an official number.

Caller ID and sender names can be spoofed, so a familiar name on screen proves nothing.

Report suspicious texts and calls to the security team just like emails. Many attacks combine channels, for example an email followed by a "helpful" phone call.
