# QR-Code Phishing (Quishing)

QR-code phishing, or quishing, hides a malicious link inside a QR code. The code may appear in an email, a PDF, a poster or a sticker placed over a genuine code, for example on a parking meter.

QR codes are risky because you cannot read the destination before scanning, and scanning moves you to a phone that may have weaker protection than your work computer.

Before opening a scanned link, check the URL preview your camera shows. Be suspicious of codes that lead to login pages, payment pages or app downloads.

Be extra careful with QR codes in unexpected emails asking you to re-authenticate, set up MFA or view a document. Legitimate IT teams rarely ask you to scan a code from an email.

Report suspicious QR-code messages and look for stickers covering official codes in public places.
