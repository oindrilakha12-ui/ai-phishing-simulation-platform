# How to Report a Suspicious Email

If an email looks suspicious, report it instead of deleting or ignoring it. Use the Report Phish page in PhishGuard: paste the full email, including headers if you can, and add a short note about what made you suspicious.

Do not click links, open attachments, reply, or forward the message to colleagues. Forwarding spreads the risk and can strip the headers the security team needs.

After you report, the security team triages the message. Its status changes from new to triaged, and then to confirmed simulation or benign. You get feedback in your in-app notifications.

If you already clicked a link or entered information, report it immediately and say so. Change the affected password from a trusted device and tell the IT help desk. Reporting quickly limits the damage. Nobody is punished for reporting a mistake.

Reporting a simulation counts as a success. The report-to-click ratio is one of the main signs of a healthy security culture.
